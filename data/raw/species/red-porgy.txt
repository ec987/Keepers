Red Porgy
Regulation Update
FWC approved rule changes for recreational harvest of Atlantic red porgy, snowy grouper and blueline tilefish to be consistent with current and pending regulations in adjacent Atlantic federal waters.
Learn More
Regulations
Gulf State Waters
Minimum Size Limit: None
Daily Bag Limit: 2 fish or 100 lbs. per person, whichever is more.
Season: Open year-round
Atlantic State Waters
Minimum Size Limit: 14" total length
Daily Bag Limit: 1 fish per person/day
Season: Open May-June
Other Regulations
Gear Requirements:
Legal Gear: hook and line, spears, gigs
Reef fish gear requirements apply
