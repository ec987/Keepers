What can I keep?

Southwest Florida Gulf coast. Snook, grouper, oysters, mussels. Pick a date and drop a pin anywhere, use GPS, type coordinates, or search a street address.

Not official and not legal advice. Not affiliated with FWC or FDACS. Rules change by executive order, and this page was last checked on 2026-10-04. Confirm with FWC before you keep anything. When unsure, this page shows the stricter rule.
